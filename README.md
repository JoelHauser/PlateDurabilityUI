# Plate Durability

See the condition of every armor plate right on the item icon. No more opening a carrier to find
out which plate is cracked.

![Plate gauges on the 6B13, Bagariy and Redut-T5](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/JoelHauser/PlateDurabilityUI/main/images/showcase.png)

## Features

**Plate gauge on the icon**
- One bar per plate slot, always in the same order: front, back, left, right.
- A thin bar underneath for the weakest soft armor insert.
- Bar height and colour follow the plate's durability: green when new, through amber, to red when nearly gone.
- A striped bar is a broken plate. A dashed outline is an empty plate slot.
- A dark cap on top of a bar marks durability lost to repairs for good, like the wear segment on weapon durability bars.
- Updates live, even while you take damage with the inventory open.

**Everywhere armor shows up**
- Your stash, your character, bodies and containers in raid, traders and the flea market.
- Body armor and armored rigs. Helmets are not covered yet.

**Detailed plate tooltip**
- Hover the gauge (or the durability numbers) for every plate and soft insert with its armor class, exact durability and percentage.
- Empty and broken slots are listed too, along with how many plate slots are filled.

**Plate break message**
- In raid, when one of your plates is shot to zero, a message tells you which one, for example "Front plate broken (Kirasa-N)".

**Compatibility**
- Works with armor and plates from other mods, with no patches or lists needed.
- Sits beside Armor Class Icon's badges, never on top of them.
- Only reads items. Nothing about your gear changes.

## Settings

Press F12 in game.

- **Style:** Smoke, Classic, Bare or Slim.
- **Corner:** bottom left, top left or top right. It moves clear of the game's own icons and text.
- **Size**, and whether bars animate when a plate is hit or swapped.
- **Slot labels:** letters (F, B, L, R) or each plate's armor class, or none.
- **Where it shows:** body armor, rigs, stash, traders, flea market, each on or off.
- **Hide on healthy armor:** only show the gauge once a plate is worn, broken or missing.
- **Full bar means:** the plate as new, or its maximum after repairs.
- Turn the soft armor bar, the tooltip and the break message on or off.

## Install

Unzip into your SPT folder. Client only, SPT 4.1.x.
